How June covers a shift.

Every open shift moves through the same path: detected, matched, contacted, confirmed. A person can step in at any point along the way.

MonitoringA shift is assigned

June watches for a callout or a missed confirmation. Nothing else happens until one shows up.

OutreachThe gap is live

June works through the bench to find a qualified replacement.

EscalatedA person takes it

Outreach pauses and the shift goes to a live scheduler with everything so far attached.

ResolvedConfirmed and logged

The caregiver is notified and the assignment is on record.

Caught before anyone notices.

A caregiver doesn't confirm

If an assigned caregiver hasn't confirmed within your set window, June lines up a backup while your scheduler follows up directly.

A callout comes in

A cancellation, a logged callout or a missed clock in moves the shift straight into outreach.

A thin bench gets flagged

If a client's pool of qualified caregivers drops below a safe level, June flags it early rather than after the fact.

Not everyone hears first.

Before anyone is contacted, June checks the basics: certification, language, any client preference, and whether the visit would push someone past an authorized hours limit. Fail one of those and a caregiver never enters the pool.

Caregivers who have worked with this client before and show up go first. Reliable caregivers new to the client come next. Everyone else hears only if those groups don't pan out, weighted by hours already worked that week and the drive to the client.

The pace follows the clock.

Critical

Starts soon

The call comes first, with a text as backup.

Every call offers a simple choice: accept, decline, or stay on the line for a person. Anything other than a clear yes or no puts a live scheduler on the phone.

Every reply is read twice.

Replies are sorted into accepted, declined, needs clarification or no response. If something looks like a yes but June isn't sure of the read, the shift doesn't lock. It's held for a scheduler to confirm.

A misread never quietly turns into an uncovered shift.
