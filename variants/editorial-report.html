Questions owners ask

Who is June for?

Home care agency owners and schedulers. If callouts land on your scheduler's phone at night and on weekends, June takes them.

What does June replace?

The phone tag after a callout: working down a list, texting one caregiver at a time, waiting, then calling the next. June starts the moment the shift opens, at any hour.

Does it work with my scheduling system?

June works with WellSky. It reads your schedule and writes the booking back. Using something else? Tell us when you join early access.

What does it cost?

Early access starts from $1,350 a month. We'll walk through your own week of callouts with you before you decide.

Will June text my caregivers without asking me?

Not until you say so. An agency starts with June reading only, then working alongside your scheduler, then with your scheduler approving each outreach, and only then on its own.

What if June can't fill a shift?

It hands the shift to your scheduler with the whole thread attached, word for word, so nobody starts over.
