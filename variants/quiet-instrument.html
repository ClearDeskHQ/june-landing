Questions owners ask.

Who is June for? Home care agency owners and schedulers who lose shifts to callouts, most of all at night, on weekends and on holidays.

Do caregivers need an app? No. June reaches them by text and by phone call, the way your office already does.

What system does June work with? WellSky. June books the caregiver who says yes and writes the assignment back. If you schedule somewhere else, tell us on the early access form.

In what order are caregivers asked? People who have worked with the client and show up go first. Reliable caregivers new to the client come next. Everyone else hears only if those groups don't pan out.

Who is never asked? Anyone missing a certification, a language or a client preference, or who would go past an authorized hours limit.

What if a reply is unclear? The shift doesn't lock. It's held for your scheduler to confirm, so a misread never turns into an uncovered visit.

What happens when June can't fill a shift? It goes to your scheduler with the whole thread attached, word for word.

Can my scheduler take over? Yes, with one click, whatever state the shift is in.

What does it cost? From $1,350 a month during early access.
