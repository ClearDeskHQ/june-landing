Privacy Policy

What JuneAI LLC collects, what it does with it, and how to stop June's texts. Last updated November 9, 2026.

Who we are

JuneAI LLC ("June", "we") makes June, software that home care agencies use to run their schedules and to text and call their caregivers and clients. We work for the agency. When June texts or calls you, it is on behalf of your agency.

The texts June sends

June sends informational texts only: open shift offers, schedule confirmations, clock-in and clock-out reminders, and updates about visits. June never sends marketing or promotional texts.

How you agree to get texts

You get texts from June only after the home care agency you work for, or receive care from, adds your mobile number to June and you have agreed to receive texts from that agency. The first text names the agency and June and tells you how to stop.

What we collect

Your name and mobile number, as your agency keeps them in its scheduling system. The texts and calls between you and June. When an agency signs up, the name and work email of the person who signs up, and a record of the agreement they accepted: its text, the date and time, and their IP address.

How we use it

We use your mobile number to text and call you about shifts, schedules and visits for your agency, and to reply when you write back. We use an agency's records only to provide June to that agency.

We do not share your mobile information

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We share your number only with the messaging provider that delivers texts on our behalf, and only so delivery can happen. Every company that handles care details for us has signed a business associate agreement under HIPAA.

Campaign source data

When you visit our website from a link that includes campaign tags (for example, from a LinkedIn post), we record anonymous campaign source data: the campaign source and name, the page you landed on, and the referring site. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or anything you enter in our forms.

Stopping the texts

Reply STOP to any text and June stops texting you. Reply HELP for help, call (877) 563-9814, or email support@junecare.ai. Opting out is handled by the carrier network as well as by us, so it takes effect whatever our records say.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep records for as long as your agency uses June and as the law requires. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email privacy@junecare.ai. A record that you agreed to or stopped texts is kept, because it is what protects you and us.
