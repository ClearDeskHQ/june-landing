Terms of Service

The terms for June's texts and calls, from JuneAI LLC. Last updated November 9, 2026.

What this program is

June is software from JuneAI LLC that home care agencies use to text and call their caregivers and clients. The texts are informational: open shift offers, schedule confirmations, clock-in and clock-out reminders, and updates about visits. June never sends marketing or promotional texts.

How you agree to get texts

Your home care agency asks first. It sends everyone a message through WellSky, its scheduling system, saying it uses June to text about shifts and visits and asking you to agree. June texts you only after you agree. The first text names the agency and June and tells you how to stop.

Messages you can expect

Message frequency varies with your schedule and how many shifts open near you. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan. We do not charge you for these messages. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Stopping or getting help

Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help, call (877) 563-9814, or email support@junecare.ai. You can start again later by telling your agency or replying START.

Your privacy

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Read the Privacy Policy for what we collect and how we use it.

Your responsibilities

Give your agency a mobile number you control, and tell them if it changes. If a number is not yours, we may text someone who never agreed to it.

Changes

We may update these terms. If we change what kind of texts we send, we will ask for your agreement again rather than relying on the agreement you gave for something else.
